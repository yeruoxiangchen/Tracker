Decision and isolation
----------------------

- Formal lane: manifest-order CFG16 indices 16-31, then final32 indices 32-63.
- Exploratory lane: CFG=5 from the old legacy-only diagnostic.  Its deployment
  remains formal=false/passed=false and records latent/count failures.
- Never reuse an exploratory support cache/checkpoint as a formal result.
- If corrected F5 selects a deployment identity different from exploratory
  CFG=5, rebuild formal F7/F8/F9/F10; no file in the exploratory directory is
  promoted or renamed.
- Every nonzero RC exits only its subshell; the caller terminal stays open.
